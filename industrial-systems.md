### Industrial Plants and Manufacturing Systems

"Makvolt Power provides a suite of advanced power protection products for Industrial Automation Equipments and Process Control Systems." ### Power Anomalies Problem Statement

Factory machinery creates electrical disturbances that cause malfunctions. Semiconductor components face reduced lifespan from small transient voltages. Motor controllers and variable speed drives frequently trip offline due to brief voltage transients.

### Solution Offered

"Makvolt Power's double conversion Online UPS systems provide the highest level of power protection removing voltage transients, sags, surges, noise, harmonics and frequency-related problems." The systems handle high harmonics from machinery while minimizing impedance.
