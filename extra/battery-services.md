## Main Heading
Battery Services

## Service Categories

### Battery Installation
"Makvolt Power's trained technicians provide battery installation service in accordance with the manufacturer's specifications." The company handles physical placement, interconnection within strings, DC cabling, and software adjustments as needed.

### Battery Servicing & Maintenance
"To maintain maximum uptime, Makvolt Power provides preventive and corrective battery maintenance and inspections at periodic intervals."

#### Service Checks Include:
- Environmental checks – ambient temperature of battery room
- Cleanliness of equipment
- Condition of battery cabinets
- Voltage readings – cell and string voltages
- Detailed examination of individual cells – post and interconnector corrosion
- Impedance/conductance measurement
- Individual battery load test – discharge test, site load and/or load banks
- Thermal imaging, specific gravity tests and level checking (wet batteries only) with top up if required

### Battery Replacement
The company assesses specific requirements and creates customized replacement programs. They design schedules to minimize business disruption and handle environmentally responsible disposal of old batteries.
