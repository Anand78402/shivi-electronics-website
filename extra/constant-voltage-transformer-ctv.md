## Main Heading
Constant Voltage Transformer (CVT)

## Product Overview
**Capacity Range:** 500 VA to 5 kVA

Makvolt's CVTs are ferroresonant devices featuring "Primary winding and Secondary winding that are separated from each other" through galvanic isolation. This design prevents direct connection between input and output, protecting sensitive equipment from electrical noise, transients, sags, surges, and brownouts. The devices can operate bidirectionally to shield the mains from noisy loads.

## Applications
- Medical Equipment
- Lab Instruments
- Sensitive electronic devices
