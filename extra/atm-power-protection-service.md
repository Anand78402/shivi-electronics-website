Makvolt Power delivers "a suite of advanced power protection products including Online Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) systems and Inverters for complete backup power for business continuity."

## Power Protection for Automatic Teller Machines (ATMs)
"ATMs are connected to both power and modem lines. Electrical and frequency interference in power lines can zap a modem connection as well as cause component failure resulting in costly downtime."

The solution involves "isolation transformer-based power conditioning technology which provides clean AC power to sensitive ATM equipments."
