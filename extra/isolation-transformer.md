## Product Overview
"An Isolation transformer is an electrical device that transfers energy between two or more circuits through electromagnetic induction and delivers a truly isolated clean power environment."

## Key Benefits
- Provides Isolation from noisy transmission line.
- Prevents Spikes and Galvanic leakages from reaching sensitive equipment.
- Provides Electrostatic Shielding.
- Types: Step-Up & Step-Down

## Features
- High quality lamination and conductors ensure Low Losses Phase
- Impregnated with International Quality Resins and Varnishes
- Chemically treated & plated hardware to avoid corrosion
- Use of higher grade insulating material & special shielding techniques to minimize transverse mode noise
- Designed with windings to reduce overall capacitance of the winding
- Terminals are shrouded for safety
- The efficiency of the transformer is high due to low dissipation of heat energy.

## Applications
- Printing Machinery
- CNC Machinery, Laser Engravers
- Textile Machinery
- Hospital Facilities
