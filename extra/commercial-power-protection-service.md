## AMC
AMC after warranty is crucial for smooth operations because the power management equipments are to be used for many years and hence require maintenance on a regular basis. This does not imply that the products will otherwise be prone to frequent breakdowns, but as solutions today power super-critical environments, products have to be maintained by predictive failure avoidance. Preventive maintenance ensures low downtime by early detection and monitoring.

Besides the cushion of preventive maintenance, Makvolt Power AMC provides the insurance of breakdown maintenance. Makvolt Power AMC provides comprehensive coverage for all parts and labour cost against any breakdown with a limited investment.

We also undertake AMC of multi-brand UPS systems after inspection by our engineers.

## AMC of Multi-brand UPS Systems
