### Who We Are
"MAKVOLT IS A RENOWNED BRAND IN THE INDIAN POWER ELECTRONICS INDUSTRY PROVIDING LEADING-EDGE POWER SOLUTIONS AT VALUE PRICES."

Makvolt Power is a leading manufacturer of uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) and power conditioning and backup products. ISO 9001: 2008 certified company offering full range of power protection products across Indian cities.

Company founded by engineers, technocrats and business entrepreneurs with 15+ years of steady growth. Led by Mr. Sandeep Agarwal, recognized for innovative power protection technology, superior quality, and customer service.

### Product Portfolio
Makvolt Power offers wide range of power conditioning and backup solutions for clean power delivery and continuous supply during outages.

**Products listed:**
- Online Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS) up to 200 kVA
- Line Interactive UPS (500 VA-3 kVA)
- Sine Wave Inverter up to 120 kVA
- Lift Inverter
- Constant Voltage Transformers (CVTs) - 150 VA to 5 kVA (1-phase) & upto 15 kVA (3-phase)
- Servo Controlled Voltage Stabilizers
- Tubular and SMF Batteries of Exide, Rocket, Amaraja & Base Terminal
- Solar Power Products
