## Power Quality Problems
Power lines conduct more than the electricity that runs your systems. They also carry electrical disturbances that can disrupt, degrade, and destroy your systems. Incoming utility power can be contaminated with spikes, surges, sags and electrical noise, causing operating problems for sensitive electronics.

Poor utility power can lead to soft errors (lock-ups, error messages, system restarts) and hard failures (board replacements, hard disk failure, component failures, improper operation of peripherals) which can ultimately shut down your system and cause havoc to your business operations.
