What We Offer

Full lifecycle support

From the first site survey to long-term maintenance, one team looks after your power system.

Site Survey & Load Assessment

Our engineers audit your site and work out the kVA and backup time you actually need.

  • Load and supply check (single / three-phase)
  • Right-sized UPS and battery recommendation
  • Free site survey on request

Installation & Commissioning

Systems are installed, commissioned and load-tested on site before hand-off.

  • Installation and wiring
  • Commissioning and full load testing
  • Hand-over and operating guidance

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled inspections catch battery degradation and fan wear before they cause a failure.

  • Battery health check
  • Fan, connection and cleaning checks
  • Performance and alarm review

24/7 Breakdown Support

A helpline that is answered around the clock, with on-site response for critical loads.

  • Call or WhatsApp any time
  • Fault diagnosis and repair
  • On-site visit arranged by our team

Battery Replacement

Replacement batteries for UPS and inverter systems, with old cells taken away.

  • Tubular and VRLA batteries
  • Battery bank sizing for your backup time
  • Safe disposal of old cells

Annual Maintenance Contracts

A yearly contract that keeps your equipment serviced and supported.

  • Scheduled preventive visits
  • Breakdown support
  • Cover agreed to suit your site

Annual Maintenance Contracts

Keep your system serviced, all year

An annual maintenance contract (AMC) covers regular preventive visits and breakdown support for your UPS, inverter, stabilizer or battery bank. We shape the contract around your equipment and how critical it is to your site.

Get an AMC quote Ask on WhatsApp

What an AMC can include

  • Preventive visitsScheduled inspections of the unit, batteries and connections.
  • Battery health checksKnow how much life is left before a failure.
  • Breakdown supportHelpline and on-site response when something goes wrong.
  • Parts & labourCover for repairs, as set out in your contract.

Number of visits, response time and parts cover are agreed with you in the contract. Ask us for a quote.
