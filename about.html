Our Story

Reliable Power. Uninterrupted Performance.

Shivi Electronics System builds Alpha, Beta and Gama series online UPS systems with one goal: keep critical loads running through every spike, surge and brownout.

Who We Are

A single, comprehensive power management manufacturer

Shivi Electronics System designs and supplies DSP-based online UPS systems — Alpha, Beta and Gama series — covering single-phase and three-phase installations from 1 kVA up to 300 kVA, alongside inverters, voltage stabilizers and industrial batteries.

We work directly with facility managers, EPC contractors and a nationwide network of channel partners to specify the right system the first time — not the most expensive one.

Technology

What's inside every Shivi UPS

01

DSP-based double conversion

Input AC is converted to controlled DC, then back to controlled AC — isolating critical loads from spikes, surges and brownouts.

02

High-frequency PWM inverter (IGBT)

Instantaneous sine-wave control gives high efficiency, low noise, a compact footprint and fast transient response.

03

DC shoot-through fault protection

Foolproof protection instantly switches off the IGBTs if both top and bottom devices are triggered together, preventing damage.

04

Independent LCD monitoring

Real-time status and alarms — input/output voltage, frequency, battery % and load % — on every unit.
