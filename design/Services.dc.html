Services

The highest standard of support, training, equipment and professional expertise — delivered by field-based engineers and technical support staff committed to your power protection systems.

01

Consulting

Pre-sales consultancy to plan your power protection requirement — load, input quality, harmonic control, redundancy, safety, space, cooling, maintenance access and future scalability.

02

Product Customization

Input range, isolation, runtime and form factor built to your specification — for new or existing setups, with earthing, cabling and electrical guidance.

03

Request a Site Survey

On-site study of load and installation conditions so the solution is sized to the room, not a datasheet.

04

UPS & Inverter Service

Installation & commissioning, training, and warranty support — module-based servicing with logistics for emergency and routine visits.

05

AMC Services

Comprehensive coverage for all parts and labour against breakdown, plus preventive maintenance for low downtime — including AMC of multi-brand UPS systems.

06

Battery Services

Installation to manufacturer spec, preventive and corrective maintenance, impedance and load testing, thermal imaging, and scheduled replacement with responsible disposal.
