About Shivi
Electronics System

A renowned name in the Indian power electronics industry, providing leading-edge power solutions at value prices — a single comprehensive provider of Total Power Management for India and overseas.

Who we are

Founded by engineers, technocrats and entrepreneurs

Shivi Electronics System is a manufacturer of uninterruptible power supplies and power conditioning and backup products, offering a full range of power protection across Indian cities.

Our ideology rests on a solid foundation of research and development, guided by a customer-first philosophy. Our solutions let customers enhance business productivity by integrating their existing setup with our robust systems.

Key strengths: cost competitiveness, product quality, and extensive experience across industries, institutions, IT, residential, telecom and healthcare sectors.
