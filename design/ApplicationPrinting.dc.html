Our approach

Online UPS Systems

Advanced digital microprocessor-based systems using SPWM technology with IGBT modules — no-break clean sine-wave power, precise voltage regulation, complete isolation from utility aberrations, redundancy, scalability and intelligent battery management.

Sine Wave Inverters

Efficient, reliable power continuity for state-of-the-art AC-powered equipment: N+1 redundancy, rack-mounting, high surge capability, independence of load power factor and noiseless operation.

Servo Controlled Voltage Stabilizer

Superior conditioning for sophisticated printing equipment against voltage-fluctuation hazards, with solid-state linear control and instantaneous response.

Solar Power Systems

A renewable, clean and cost-effective energy source for printing applications — high conversion efficiency, long product life and low maintenance from superior design.
